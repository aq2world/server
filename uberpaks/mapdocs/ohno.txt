* Description *

A wooded mountain at night. A central massif with ridges running out
to the corners, an east bluff and a north ridge for the long shots,
and a lake in the west with a timber bridge across it. Crawl through
the mossy cave, warm up in the palju outside the log cabin, or climb
into the hot air balloon.

And watch the sky. The saucer beams up anyone who stands under it.
