* Description *

A city block after dark. The cinema is still lit and the arcade is still
open, but the good routes are the ones nobody queues for: service alleys
behind the storefronts, ivy on the back of the buildings, and the
rooftops above them.

Three levels, all worth holding. Street for the long shots, alleys and
interiors for the close work, rooftops for the players who know where the
ladders come out. Nothing up there is safe for long.
