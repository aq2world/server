* LEVEL DESCRIPTION *
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Oh no, you're TINY! Inspired by the old map huge, you're put in an 
Actionquaker's gaming room while being shrunk down. Map is jumpy and fast paced,
with many easter eggs to some of the active player base during the making of the
map.
Hopefully you enjoy it while being tiny!
