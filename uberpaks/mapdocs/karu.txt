aq2world's winter mapjam 2026 (not a competition)
"make a winter themed retro map with satisying jumps" 

well, it is a winter map. thats for sure.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

when this jam was announced i didnt even feel like making anything at 
first. then i got some weird inspiration about a ski resort and one 
thing led to another. the map went through a lot of changes and total 
overhauls during development and the final layout only started coming 
together at the very last minute. oh well good thing this wasnt a 
competition

i tried to get a retro vibe of some really bleak and depressing ski 
resort thats why the name is karu. its finnish and means bleak or rugged. 
which this map totally is.

the map is pretty big and open so bots are probably gonna die just by 
running off the edge lol. r_speeds peak around 3000 near the halfpipe 
but otherwise wpoly is like 1500 to 2000 sometimes even lower

custom textures made and edited with paint.net and wally.
that table with the open umbrella was inspired by wizardext from bwcity. 
still made it myself. 

deadline came way faster than i thought so i had to rush everything. 
been pulling 12 hour shifts at work and only had like 4 weeks to make 
this so yeah.. if u feel like modding the map and fixing stuff go right 
ahead. hmu on discord and i can send u the map files

i think karu is still playable. happy holidays and hope u enjoy this
 
thanks,
iLPo
